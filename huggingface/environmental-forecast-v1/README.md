# Environmental Forecasting v1

**Every reading in this dataset is synthetic.** SafeSense generated these time series and published them here. They are not captured BME680 or MQ-135 measurements, observed incidents, or an independent real-world validation set. The project's separate Live Hardware demonstration reads a physical ESP32; those readings are not rows in this dataset and were not used to train or evaluate the released forecast model.

## Contents and split

The dataset has 760 episodes of 151 one-minute samples each (114,760 rows). The training split has 600 episodes and 90,600 rows from 10 scenario families. The test split has 160 episodes and 24,160 rows from 8 *different* families. Episodes stay intact across splits; do not randomly split overlapping windows from one episode between training and test.

Each row contains `episode_id`, `split`, `scenario_family`, `scenario_key`, `room`, `minute`, and five simulated channels:

| Channel | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `temperature_c` | BME680-like temperature in °C |
| `humidity_pct` | BME680-like relative humidity in % |
| `pressure_pa` | BME680-like barometric pressure in Pa |
| `gas_resistance_ohm` | Non-specific BME680-like gas resistance in Ω |
| `mq135_adc_raw` | MQ-135-like raw ADC value; not gas concentration or CO₂ ppm |

Rooms are Cold storage, Laboratory, Classroom, Bakery, and Server room. Scenario names describe the simulator's intended situation; they are **not observed activity or incident labels**.

## How the readings were generated

The seeded generator in `source/forecast_scenarios.py` creates coupled sensor-like trajectories. It varies baseline, disturbance onset, amplitude, response speed, noise, weather drift, and temporary recovery. The five channel values are generated by formulas, not copied from physical sensor logs. `source/export_forecast_dataset.py` produced the exact `source/forecast_synthetic_v1.csv.gz` export; the train and test Parquet files contain the same rows. `source/dataset_manifest.json` records the scenario families and demonstration ranges.

Published guidance supports the *choice of situations*, not the values or accuracy of the generated trajectories:

| Scenario theme | Operational source |
| --- | --- |
| Cold-storage door opening and temperature excursions | [USDA FSIS, Freezing and Food Safety](https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/freezing-and-food-safety) |
| Laboratory ventilation and contaminant control | [OSHA, Occupational exposure to hazardous chemicals in laboratories](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.1450) |
| Classroom temperature, humidity, and ventilation | [US EPA, Reference Guide for Indoor Air Quality in Schools](https://www.epa.gov/iaq-schools/reference-guide-indoor-air-quality-schools) |
| Indoor workplace heat | [CDC/NIOSH, Heat Stress and Workers](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/topics/heatstress/default.html) |
| Data-center cooling and airflow recirculation | [US DOE, Best Practices Guide for Energy-Efficient Data Center Design](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-07/best-practice-guide-data-center-design_0.pdf) |

The [Bosch BME680 datasheet](https://www.bosch-sensortec.com/media/boschsensortec/downloads/datasheets/bst-bme680-ds001.pdf) describes its temperature, humidity, pressure, and broad gas-resistance signals. Gas resistance is affected by factors including humidity. Neither it nor an uncalibrated raw MQ-135 ADC reading identifies a chemical or proves a concentration in ppm.

## Row timing and preparation

`minute` is a relative index from 0 through 150 at one-minute spacing, not a wall-clock timestamp. No physical sensor placement, hardware collection date, or measured sampling interval is attached to these rows.

The generator returns five `float32` values per minute. It bounds humidity to 5–95%, gas resistance to 1,000–300,000 Ω, and MQ-135 raw ADC to 0–4,095; temperature and pressure are not clipped. The CSV export rounds values to four decimal places. Before writing Zstandard-compressed train/test Parquet files, the packaging step checks column names, missing values, duplicate episode/minute pairs, complete 151-row episodes, split sizes, and disjoint scenario families. The dataset packaging does not impute missing values, smooth readings, remove outliers, or normalize channels. The forecasting trainer fits its own standardization on training windows only; that transformation is not applied to the published rows.

## Intended use and limits

The project task observes up to 60 minutes of past readings and predicts the five channels at +5, +10, +15, +20, +25, and +30 minutes. Hidden simulator parameters, scenario keys, risk labels, and future values must not be used as model inputs. The supplied scenario fields are for auditing and split control.

This dataset supports a reproducible **synthetic demonstration** and tests on unseen *synthetic* scenario families. It does not establish performance in a real room, under real sensor drift, or during a real hazard. Demonstrating those claims requires independently recorded physical histories and future outcomes, evaluated on sessions resembling the intended deployment. See [NIST AI RMF, Measure 2](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) for the distinction between test evidence and deployment-setting validation.
